SHRAVAN logo - final (direction A, three ripples)
Finalised 31 Oct 2026

THE IDEA
Three arcs forming an ear, closing in on a point: listen, reflect, apply
(sravana, manana, nididhyasana). The dot is the idea taken in.

FILES
SVG/        Vector masters. Wordmark is outlined (no font needed).
PNG/        Transparent, large (mark 2000 px, horizontal 3000 px, stacked 2000 px).
App-Icons/  Basic (cream) and Peacock (teal) sets.

Colourways
  Basic    navy arcs, saffron dot, navy word  - on cream or white
  Peacock  emerald arcs, gold dot, ivory word - on deep teal
  Navy     one colour                         - print, stamps, light grounds
  White    one colour                         - photos, navy or dark grounds

COLOURS
  Navy    #1B2340     Saffron #E39B3B     Cream  #F8F5EF
  Teal    #08272C     Emerald #2FBF8F     Gold   #E6B954     Ivory #F2EEE3

TYPE
  Wordmark: Marcellus, tracked 0.28 em (outlined in the files).

APP ICONS (each set)
  icon-512.png, icon-192.png  web app manifest, purpose "any"   -> public/icon-512.png, public/icon-192.png
  maskable-512.png            web app manifest, purpose "maskable" (Android crops it to a circle or squircle)
  apple-touch-icon.png        180 px, iPhone / iPad home screen
  play-store-512.png          Google Play listing icon
  favicon.ico, favicon-32/16  browser tab (heavier strokes so they stay clear when small)
  icon.svg                    1024 px vector master of the icon

RULES
  - Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the inner arc.
  - Smallest sizes: mark 16 px (use the favicon files); horizontal logo 120 px wide.
  - Don't recolour outside the four colourways, stretch, rotate or re-space the letters.
  - "by AAYAMIK" is not part of the logo; it sits as a separate line in the app.
